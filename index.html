FAQ

Before you bring in a computer.

Are diagnostics really free?

Yes. Benchline's standard initial diagnostic is free. If troubleshooting requires unusually extensive testing or specialized work beyond the standard diagnostic scope, you'll be contacted before any chargeable work begins.

Will you repair something without asking me?

No. Chargeable work requires customer authorization.

Do your prices include parts?

No. Listed service prices represent labor unless specifically stated otherwise. Required parts are quoted separately.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes. Customer-supplied parts are welcome, though compatibility and condition must be verified before installation.

Do you work on Macs?

Basic diagnosis and certain software or component services may be considered case-by-case. Advanced Mac logic-board repair is not currently offered.

Can you recover files from a broken hard drive?

Basic data transfer from functioning storage is available. Physical drive recovery and advanced data-recovery services are not currently offered.

Will my files be safe?

Reasonable care is taken to protect customer data, but hardware failure and repair work can involve data-loss risk. Customers should maintain backups of important data whenever possible.
